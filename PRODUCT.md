# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro static site with local image assets and minimal client-side JavaScript.

## Users

Women in Porto looking for a trusted salon experience, especially for balayage, extensions, colour work, and restorative hair care. They need to understand the salon's expertise quickly and contact the team from a phone.

## Product Purpose

Present Lurdes Marques Cabeleireiros as a welcoming, specialist salon in Antas and make it easy for a new client to discover services, see real work, and start a WhatsApp booking conversation.

## Positioning

A Porto salon founded by Lurdes Marques in 2000, combining personal care with specialist expertise in Balayage and Extensões, plus hair treatments and manicure/pedicure services.

## Operating Context

The primary visit is mobile. Visitors browse on the way to deciding whether to contact the salon. The landing page should surface real portfolio work, service categories, location, phone, WhatsApp, and social channels without requiring a long form.

## Product Principles

- Show the work before asking for the booking.
- Make specialist care feel personal, not clinical.
- Keep the path from inspiration to WhatsApp one tap away.
- Use real proof and avoid unsupported commercial claims.
